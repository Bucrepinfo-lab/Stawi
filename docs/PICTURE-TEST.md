# Picture test with members (standing rule 19)

Purpose: check that people who read little understand each tab picture
without its words. Ten to fifteen members from at least two groups, mixed
ages, including officials and ordinary members.

## How
1. Show `design/stawi-tab-pictures.svg` on a phone or printed, one picture at a
   time, words hidden.
2. Ask, in the member's language: "What do you think you will find if you press
   this?" Do not help or correct. Write the answer down as said.
3. Then show the picture with its word and ask: "Does the picture fit the word?"

## Scoring
- Right: the answer matches the tab (see table), in any wording.
- Near: the right area but vague ("money things" for Books).
- Wrong or "I don't know".

| Picture | Tab (English / Kiswahili) | Counts as right |
|---|---|---|
| Members round a pot | My group / Kikundi | the group, chama, members, our savings group |
| Seedling on a coin | Ideas / Mawazo | growing money, a business, something to start |
| Open book and coins | Books / Hesabu | records, accounts, writing money down |
| Speech bubble | Messages / Ujumbe | messages, talking, SMS, WhatsApp |
| Megaphone | Share / Shiriki | telling others, announcing, inviting |

A picture passes when at least two in three members answer Right. A picture
that fails is redrawn and tested again before it ships widely. Record the
date, the groups, the count per picture and any redraw in `MEMORY.md`.
