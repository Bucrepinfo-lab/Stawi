# The books for any business — what is there, what is missing (20/11/26)

Owner order, 20/11/26: the books must be sellable on their own to a shop, a
butchery, a bookshop, a hotel, a pub or a guest house, with no group, and
"everything expected" must be in that section. This is the honest audit.

## Open today
A business can now be started with no group (`/groups/new` → "A business — the
books only"); its books open at once.

| Expected in a bookkeeping tool | In Stawi today |
|---|---|
| Write a sale, a purchase, an expense | Yes |
| A receipt for a sale, with how it was paid (cash, M-Pesa, card, bank) | Yes |
| Profit and loss by day, week, month and year | Yes |
| Money statement by day, week and month, checked | Yes (this week's work) |
| Tax estimates (VAT and levies) | Kenya only |
| Download the ledger, stock and tax lines (CSV) | Yes |
| Stock list with fast, slow and dead stock | Reads stock; see gaps |
| Low-stock alerts and a call-the-supplier link | Yes |
| Advice from the figures | Yes, rule-based |

## Missing — to build, in this order
1. **Add and edit stock from the screen.** Today stock comes from set-up data; an owner cannot add an item, change a price or count stock.
2. **Money owed to the business (customers on credit)** and **money the business owes (suppliers)**, with due dates. A butchery and a pub live on these.
3. **Separate business purse**: the owner's own takings kept apart from the business (already on the build list).
4. **Expense kinds** (rent, wages, power, licences, transport) so costs can be read by kind.
5. **Staff and pay**: staff list, wages, PAYE and levies per pay run (the PAYE bands are in the engine but not on the screen).
6. **More than one seller**: each sale tagged to the person who made it; end-of-shift cash count.
7. **Printed statements**: CSV downloads exist; add a statement an owner can print or save as PDF.
8. **Kinds of business**: rooms and nights for a guest house, tabs for a pub, tables for a hotel kitchen, weight for a butchery. Start with butchery and pub.
9. **Tax for other countries**, each verified against its own authority before it is shown.
10. **KRA eTIMS**: the engine drafts an invoice for demonstration only; real submission needs KRA onboarding. Owner decision.
11. **Bank and M-Pesa statement matching** (upload a statement, match it to the books).

Nothing in this list is claimed on any screen.
